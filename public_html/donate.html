Your support is crucial in ensuring education for all!

Childhood is said to be the best part of human life - an age where we are carefree, safe and happy. But a happy childhood is not the reality for many children, who are out of school. There are many reasons for children to drop out of school - from challenging socio-economic circumstances, to lack of awareness in communities where education is not seen as a priority.

Going to school not only ensures a dignified future and a happy present for children, but also gives them a safe space to express themselves, learn, share and grow. Children who drop out often have to work as child labour, are pushed into child marriages, or become victims of child trafficking.

INSC Foundation, through its 'Shiksha Na Ruke' initiative has been helping children from difficult circumstances continue their education, with the hope of a brighter future and better life. At present, we are directly providing education to over 120,000 children in 27 states of India.

Despite all the odds, these young champions have not given up and continue to dream and work hard. With your support, we can fuel their dreams with accessible and quality education. Join hands with us to ensure a happy and safe childhood for all!

Our Impact Pillars 🌟

Key Interventions Under Shiksha Na Ruke

Comprehensive educational initiatives designed to transform lives and build a brighter future for children across India.

Pillar 01 • Schooling

Primary & Secondary Education

Ensuring uninterrupted learning, free textbooks, and uniforms for underprivileged children across India.

Pillar 02 • Healthcare

Health Check-ups & Nutrition

Regular medical check-ups, growth monitoring, and wholesome nutrition to keep young learners healthy.

Pillar 03 • Pedagogy

Teacher Training & Capacity

Empowering educators with modern pedagogy, interactive digital tools, and empathetic student mentoring.

Pillar 04 • Livelihood

Vocational & Skill Training

Industry-aligned vocational programs and hands-on technical modules for youth empowerment and careers.

Pillar 05 • Wellbeing

Music, Sports & Life Skills

Promoting holistic socio-emotional health, athletic teamwork, creative arts, and mental resilience.

Pillar 06 • Infrastructure

Infrastructure & STEM Labs

Revitalizing school campuses with smart interactive classrooms, science STEM kits, and sanitation.

Pillar 07 • Inclusion

Digital Literacy Access

Connecting rural, tribal, and remote schools with digital devices, solar power, and e-learning hubs.

Pillar 08 • Empowerment

Girl Child Scholarships

Dedicated merit scholarships and career mentoring ensuring bright young girls achieve their aspirations.
