    What Are Cookies

    Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device when visiting a website. These files help websites recognize returning users and remember certain preferences.

    Note: Cookies are harmless and do not contain viruses or malicious software. They simply help improve your browsing experience.

    How We Use Cookies

    Cookies may be used for purposes such as:

    • Understanding visitor behaviour and website usage
    • Improving website functionality
    • Remembering user preferences and settings
    • Enhancing overall user experience

    Managing Cookies

    Most web browsers allow users to control cookies through their browser settings. Users can choose to disable cookies; however, some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

    Browser Settings: You can typically find cookie settings in your browser's privacy or security section. Consult your browser's help documentation for specific instructions.
