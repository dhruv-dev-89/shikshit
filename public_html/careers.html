✨ Our Culture & Mission

Why work with Shikshit

We are on a mission to democratize quality STEM education across India. Join a community of educators, mentors, and innovators building lasting change.

Student Mentorship

Empower children from underserved communities through personalized guidance, interactive workshops, and positive role modeling.

Educational Equity

Tackle systemic divides by bringing localized, vernacular, high-quality STEM and foundational learning to schools that need it most.

Community Impact

Collaborate with grassroots partners, local educators, and families to create sustainable, community-rooted educational change.

Growth & Learning

Develop your leadership, curriculum, and operational skills in an agile, supportive environment that invests in your future.
