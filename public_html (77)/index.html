Flagship Scholarship Program

National Scholarship – Merit and Assistance Scheme (NS-MAS)

NS-MAS is an established scholarship initiative that recognizes academic merit and provides financial assistance to students facing economic constraints. Open for Class 9 through Postgraduate students across India.

Visit NS-MAS Portal How It Works (4 Steps) Eligibility Criteria

Key Program Features

  • Class 9, 10, 11, 12, UG & PG eligible
  • Merit-based and financial-need evaluation
  • Transparent online registration & verification
  • Official Certificates of Merit awarded
