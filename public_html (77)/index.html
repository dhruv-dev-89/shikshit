Our Mission 🎓

Our Mission

Empowering Students with INSC — Indian National Science Congress

The Shiksht is dedicated to transforming the landscape of education across India by fostering a collaborative and innovative educational environment.

Our mission is to elevate the quality of education through the promotion of best practices, cutting-edge teaching methodologies, and the sharing of resources among member schools.

In addition, the Shiksht emphasizes the importance of holistic student development, ensuring that academic achievement is complemented by social, emotional, and physical growth.

Our Vision 🚀

Our Vision

The vision of the Shiksht is to create a dynamic and inclusive educational ecosystem where every student has access to high-quality, equitable education and every school is empowered to excel.

Our goal is to cultivate well-rounded, forward-thinking students who are prepared to thrive in an increasingly globalized world, while ensuring that each school and educator has the tools and support needed to contribute to this transformative vision.

About NS-MAS

National Scholarship – Merit and Assistance Scheme

The National Scholarship – Merit and Academic Scheme is a scholarship initiative designed to support students in need, with the mission to empower and uplift the future of students by providing essential financial assistance.

Who Can Apply?

While primarily helping unprivileged students, we're also open to:

✓ Students requiring immediate educational funds
✓ Students facing family financial crises
✓ Students with outstanding academic performance but lacking financial support
✓ If you are unsuccessful in your exam attempt, you will be eligible to reapply after a mandatory waiting period of 30 days.

Free Registration & Participation

🎯
