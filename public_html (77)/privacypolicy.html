Privacy Policy

At National Scholarship – Merit and Assistance Scheme (NS-MAS), we’re committed to empowering students across India to achieve their dreams through accessible education. Part of that commitment is protecting your privacy and keeping your personal information safe. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website (www.nsmass.org), apply for scholarships, or interact with our services.

1. Who We Are

We’re NS-MAS, a scholarship initiative dedicated to supporting students from Class 9 to post-graduation with financial aid and career guidance. Our mission is to remove financial barriers to education, and we handle your data with the same care we put into helping you succeed.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Details: Name, email, phone number, date of birth, address.
  • Academic Information: School, grades, or courses.
  • Financial Information: Family’s financial details (excluding sensitive payment info).
  • Documents: Scanned IDs, certificates, etc.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser, device info, usage patterns.
  • Communication Data: Messages via contact forms or support channels.

3. How We Collect Your Information

  • Directly from you via forms, registrations, support contact.
  • Automatically through cookies and analytics.
  • From third parties (schools, partners) with your consent.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Processing scholarship applications.
  • Personalizing your user experience.
  • Improving website usability.
  • Communicating updates, inquiries, notifications.
  • Ensuring fraud prevention and legal compliance.

5. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption (SSL) during transmission.
  • Secure server storage.
  • Limited access to authorized personnel only.
  • Regular security audits.

6. Who We Share Your Data With

  • Educational partners or sponsors (with consent).
  • Service providers (email, analytics) with confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements (court orders, etc.).
  • Anonymous data for research or reports.

7. Your Rights Over Your Data

You can request access, corrections, deletion, restrict processing, or opt-out of communications by emailing us at privacy@nsmass.org. We'll respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

  • Essential Cookies: For basic site functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: To track and improve user experience.
  • Preference Cookies: To remember your settings.

9. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Application Data: Retained for 3 years or until deletion request.
  • Website Usage Data: 12 months retention.
  • Legal requirements may extend retention periods.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain external links. Please review their privacy policies separately as we are not responsible for their practices.

11. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as needed. You'll be notified via email or website notices. Last updated: July 22, 2025.
